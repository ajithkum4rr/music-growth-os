# Project Starter

Start here for the first execution cycle.

## First 10 tasks

1. Read `README.md`, `MASTER-PLAN.md`, and `ROADMAP.md`.
2. Open `docs/00-foundation/README.md` and complete Phase 0.
3. Follow `docs/00-foundation/01-account-setup.md` to create and secure your accounts.
4. Complete `docs/01-brand/01-positioning.md` with an artist positioning statement.
5. Complete `docs/01-brand/02-visual-direction.md` with your visual mood and style.
6. Define 3 audience archetypes in `docs/02-audience-research/01-archetypes.md`.
7. Map the top platforms for those audiences in `docs/02-audience-research/02-platform-map.md`.
8. Inventory your release assets in `docs/00-foundation/03-release-inventory.md`.
9. Build a simple landing page and email signup using Phase 5 guidance.
10. Start tracking the first metrics in `/analytics` and `/data`.

## Notes

- The structure is in place through Phase 26.
- The remaining work is execution: your real music, real audience research, and real campaign data.
- Keep a clean, up-to-date record in this repo as you go.
