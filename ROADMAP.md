# ROADMAP

A practical rollout plan for `music-growth-os`.

## Goal

Build a sustainable artist growth system for `ajkay` in a structured way, starting with foundation and moving into execution.

## Timeline overview

- Week 1: Foundation, accounts, brand, audience research
- Week 2: Release preparation, digital infrastructure, content plan
- Week 3: Organic distribution, Spotify setup, landing page, email capture
- Week 4: Begin Meta ads, audience building, playlist outreach, PR research
- Week 5: Release campaign execution and analytics
- Week 6: Optimization, retargeting, fan retention, post-release review

## Weekly focus

### Week 1 — Foundation & research

- Complete `Phase 0` foundation checklist.
- Define artist identity and positioning.
- Collect audience research for the first release.
- Set up the documentation structure.

### Week 2 — Preparation & infrastructure

- Finalize release plan and music assets.
- Create content ideas and a content engine.
- Set up Spotify for Artists and necessary platform accounts.
- Build a simple landing page and email signup.

### Week 3 — Organic launch

- Publish content for the upcoming release.
- Share music with your initial network.
- Start collecting email subscribers.
- Begin tracking performance on Spotify and socials.

### Week 4 — Paid & outreach

- Start small Meta ad experiments.
- Build cold and warm audience segments.
- Research legitimate playlist curators and press targets.
- Run a first round of outreach using templates.

### Week 5 — Release execution

- Launch the release with coordinated content, paid ads, and outreach.
- Monitor early metrics and track campaign performance.
- Capture new listeners and channel them to the landing page.

### Week 6 — Optimization & review

- Review what worked and what did not.
- Pause or pivot low-performing campaigns.
- Improve creative, landing pages, and audience targeting.
- Document campaign lessons and update `analytics`.

## Quick start

1. Review `MASTER-PLAN.md`.
2. Follow the `checklists/foundation-checklist.md`.
3. Open `docs/00-foundation/README.md`.
4. Create the first release plan in `/campaigns`.

## Repository health

Keep this repo current by adding:

- campaign summaries in `/campaigns`
- results and metrics in `/analytics`
- experiment notes in `/experiments`
- outreach tracking in `/data`
- operational checklists in `/checklists`

## Notes

- This roadmap is a guideline, not a strict deadline.
- Adjust based on your schedule, budget, and release timeline.
- Always verify current platform UI and policy changes.
