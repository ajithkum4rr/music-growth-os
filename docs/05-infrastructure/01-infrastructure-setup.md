# Phase 5 — Infrastructure Setup

## Goal

Create the tracking, asset, and automation infrastructure needed to support your launch.

## Why this matters

Good infrastructure makes reporting easier and prevents messy asset management as the launch progresses.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — centralize assets

Create folders for:
- Audio and stems
- Artwork and visuals
- Social and video assets
- Pitch materials and contacts
- Analytics notes

### Step 2 — set up tracking

Record your UTM naming rules and traffic sources:
- Source: instagram, tiktok, email, ads
- Medium: organic, paid, email
- Campaign: release-name, prelaunch, launch

### Step 3 — connect analytics

If you have a landing page or website, add tracking:
- Google Analytics / GA4
- Link tracking for Spotify and socials
- Email form conversion tracking

### Step 4 — create shared notes

Build simple documentation for:
- Account URLs and profile links
- Platform access notes
- Release asset status

## What to record

- Folder roadmap
- Tracking setup details
- UTM naming conventions
- Account and login notes (no passwords)

## Common mistakes

- Saving assets in too many places.
- Using inconsistent naming.
- Skipping tracking until after launch.

## Notes

- Use a tool you can access from anywhere.
- Keep the infrastructure lean at first.
