## Goal

Define the identity, tone, and public positioning for `ajkay`.

## Why this matters

A strong brand makes your music easier to recognize, recommend, and remember.
It also helps you create consistent content and clear marketing messages.

## What this includes

- Artist story and values
- Genre positioning and audience fit
- Visual style and creative direction
- Messaging and tagline
