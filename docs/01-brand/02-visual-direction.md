# Phase 1 — Visual Direction

## Goal

Define the visual style and creative direction for `ajkay`.

## Why this matters

Visuals are often the first thing people see. They must fit your music and make your brand recognizable.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — choose your visual mood

Pick 3–5 adjectives that describe the visual mood.
Examples:
- moody
- cinematic
- minimal
- vibrant
- nostalgic
- futuristic

### Step 2 — define color and imagery

Choose a simple palette:
- primary color
- secondary color
- accent color

Choose imagery themes:
- city nights
- studio lights
- nature textures
- abstract shapes

### Step 3 — set typography and video style

Choose one or two font styles:
- modern sans serif
- handwritten script
- minimal display

Choose video formats:
- lyric visuals
- behind-the-scenes clips
- performance slices
- motion graphics

### Step 4 — create a brand notes page

Capture:
- mood adjectives
- color palette
- imagery themes
- typography notes
- example artists, videos, and posts

## What to record

- Visual mood words.
- Color palette and imagery direction.
- Video style guidelines.
- Example references.

## Common mistakes

- Choosing too many visual directions.
- Mixing unrelated aesthetics.
- Copying an artist’s exact style instead of adapting it.

## Notes

- Keep visuals consistent across platforms.
- Use this guide when making cover art, social posts, and videos.
- For early MVP, a simple clean style is better than a complex one.
