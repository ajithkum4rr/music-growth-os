# Phase 3 — Release Timeline & Checklist

## Goal

Build a clear timeline and readiness checklist for the release.

## Why this matters

A release can slip when tasks are unclear. A timeline keeps the launch aligned and helps you hit deadlines.

## Timeline framework

### 8 weeks out
- confirm release date
- finalize artwork and metadata
- build landing page and email signup
- choose distribution channels

### 6 weeks out
- draft press outreach and playlist lists
- plan content around release story
- prepare paid media strategy

### 4 weeks out
- open pre-save or teaser campaign
- begin audience outreach
- test landing page and tracking

### 2 weeks out
- finalize all creative and content
- schedule emails and social posts
- brief collaborators and media contacts

### release week
- launch landing page
- send release announcement
- begin paid and organic promotion
- monitor analytics continuously

## Readiness checklist

- [ ] release date confirmed
- [ ] track artwork + metadata approved
- [ ] distribution assets uploaded
- [ ] landing page ready
- [ ] email capture live
- [ ] press and playlist outreach drafted
- [ ] content scheduled
- [ ] ad campaigns set up
- [ ] analytics tracking enabled
- [ ] launch day in calendar

## Notes

- Adjust the timeline for shorter release cycles.
- Keep the checklist updated as tasks finish.
