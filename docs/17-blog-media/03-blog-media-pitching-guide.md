# Phase 17 — Blog & Media Pitching Guide

## Goal

Pitch your release to bloggers, playlists, and media with a clear outreach approach.

## Why this matters

A strong pitch improves your chances of coverage and helps your story stand out.

## Pitch preparation

### 1. research targets

Find outlets that cover artists like you and audiences who care about your sound.

### 2. personalize the outreach

Mention a recent story, playlist, or theme that fits your release.

### 3. keep the pitch concise

Share the key details quickly:
- who you are
- what you released
- why it matters
- what you want from them

### 4. provide support assets

Include links to:
- music preview
- artist bio
- press images
- release notes

## Follow-up plan

- wait 3–5 days for a response
- send one polite follow-up
- note any feedback for future pitches

## Checklist

- [ ] target list researched
- [ ] outreach template prepared
- [ ] assets included
- [ ] custom note added for each contact
- [ ] follow-up scheduled

## Notes

- Treat media outreach as a relationship-building process.
- Keep your pitch useful, not promotional.
