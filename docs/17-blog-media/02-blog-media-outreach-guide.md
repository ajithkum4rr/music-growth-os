# Phase 17 — Blog / Media Outreach Guide

## Goal

Plan and execute outreach to blogs, podcasts, and media outlets effectively.

## Why this matters

Media coverage boosts credibility and helps your release reach new audiences.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — choose the right outlets

Target publications that match your genre, story, and audience.

### Step 2 — personalize your outreach

Mention specifics about the outlet’s recent coverage or audience.

### Step 3 — keep the pitch concise

Include:
- release headline
- artist story
- why it fits the outlet
- preview links

### Step 4 — track outreach status

Record:
- outreach date
- response status
- follow-up timing
- results

## What to record

- list of outlet targets
- pitch approach
- response notes

## Common mistakes

- sending generic outreach.
- pitching irrelevant outlets.
- not following up once courteously.

## Notes

- Keep media outreach respectful and relevant.
- Focus on a handful of good targets rather than mass emails.
