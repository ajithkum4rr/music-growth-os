# Phase 17 — Blog / Media Plan

## Goal

Plan how to secure earned media coverage and share it effectively.

## Why this matters

Earned coverage can increase reach and introduce your music to new audiences with credibility.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — choose opportunities

Identify 5–10 media targets that align with your sound.
Include:
- blogs
- online magazines
- playlists with editorial blogs
- local press

### Step 2 — craft the value proposition

Explain why your release matters to their audience.
Use:
- unique story angle
- release relevance
- audience fit

### Step 3 — track outreach

Record:
- target name
- contact info
- outreach date
- follow-up schedule

### Step 4 — share coverage

Once published:
- share the coverage on socials
- add it to your press kit
- note referral traffic if available

## What to record

- media target list
- outreach notes
- published coverage links

## Common mistakes

- Seeking coverage without a clear angle.
- Chasing press that does not match your audience.
- Not using coverage in future promotions.

## Notes

- Keep the list manageable and relevant.
- Add coverage to your marketing assets and bio.
