## Goal

Leverage media coverage, blogs, and publicity to amplify your reach.

## Why this matters

Earned media adds credibility and can drive discovery from new audiences.

## Prerequisites

- PR outreach in progress
- Release story
- Media targets

## Accounts & tools

- Media tracker
- Press kit or release note
- Email communication

## Estimated cost

- FREE to start.

## Time required

- 4–6 hours to identify and pitch media targets.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — identify publicity opportunities

- blogs and online magazines
- podcasts
- YouTube shows
- radio and playlists
- interviews and premieres

### Step 2 — evaluate opportunities

Choose opportunities with:
- relevant audiences
- editorial quality
- real engagement

### Step 3 — prepare your media assets

Have ready:
- press kit or one-sheet
- links to music and socials
- artist bio and story

### Step 4 — pitch and follow up

Send a tailored pitch and track responses.

### Step 5 — promote coverage

- share features on socials
- add press to your landing page
- thank the media outlet

## What you should see

- A publicity target list.
- A press kit draft.
- Published coverage tracked.

## What to record

- Opportunities pursued.
- Coverage outcomes.
- Traffic or audience impact.

## Metrics

- Media placements
- Referral traffic
- Social or streaming spikes

## Common errors

- Chasing vanity outlets.
- Forgetting to promote earned coverage.
- Not following up politely.

## Troubleshooting

- If a pitch is ignored, refine the hook.
- If coverage is weak, focus on more relevant outlets.
- If a media outlet requires payment, vet it carefully.

## Notes

- Publicity is often slower than ads but valuable for credibility.
- Track the actual impact, not just the placement.
