# Phase 18 — Collaboration Plan

## Goal

Identify collaboration opportunities that can expand your audience and creative reach.

## Why this matters

Collaborations help you reach new fans and can create stronger content than solo efforts alone.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — list potential collaborators

Consider:
- fellow artists
- producers
- visual creators
- playlist curators

### Step 2 — define the value exchange

For each collaborator, note what you offer and what you need:
- creative input
- audience exposure
- cross-promotion

### Step 3 — create collaboration ideas

Write a brief concept for each:
- co-release or feature
- remix or rework
- joint social content
- shared playlist or newsletter mention

### Step 4 — plan outreach

Record:
- collaborator name
- contact method
- pitch idea
- follow-up date

## What to record

- collaborator shortlist
- collaboration concepts
- outreach notes

## Common mistakes

- Choosing collaborators only by follower count.
- Offering vague value.
- Reaching out without a clear idea.

## Notes

- Focus on collaborators who share a similar audience and creative fit.
- Keep conversations transparent and professional.
