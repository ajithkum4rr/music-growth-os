## Goal

Build collaborations and relationships that extend your audience.

## Why this matters

Collaborations allow you to share audiences, learn from peers, and create new content together.

## Prerequisites

- A clear artist identity
- Release plan or content ideas

## Accounts & tools

- Contact tracker
- Outreach templates
- Collaboration pitch notes

## Estimated cost

- FREE to start.
- PAID only if a collaboration involves production or services.

## Time required

- 4–8 hours to identify partners and begin outreach.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — identify collaboration types

- co-writes or features
- remix swaps
- joint content or livestreams
- playlist or promo support

### Step 2 — choose partners

- similar genre or mood
- complementary audience size
- aligned content style

### Step 3 — define value exchange

- what you offer
- what they gain
- timing and deliverables

### Step 4 — pitch the collaboration

Keep it:
- short
- specific
- clear about why it makes sense

### Step 5 — track the project

Document:
- partner name
- goal
- timeline
- outcome

## What you should see

- A collaboration target list.
- A partnership pitch.
- A project tracking entry.

## What to record

- Contact details.
- Collaboration terms.
- Results and follow-through.

## Metrics

- New audience reach
- Content engagement
- Cross-promotion results

## Common errors

- Choosing partners only by follower count.
- Offering unclear value.
- Failing to follow through.

## Troubleshooting

- If a partner is unresponsive, move on.
- If the collaboration is unfocused, rewrite the shared goal.
- If the outcome is weak, debrief what can improve.

## Notes

- Collaborations are relationships first, projects second.
- Keep communication clear and respectful.
