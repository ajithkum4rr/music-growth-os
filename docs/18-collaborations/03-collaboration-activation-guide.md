# Phase 18 — Collaboration Activation Guide

## Goal

Activate collaborations so they help amplify the release and connect with new fans.

## Why this matters

Collaborations are more effective when they are intentionally promoted and aligned with your release goals.

## Activation steps

### 1. set shared goals

Agree with collaborators on what success looks like:
- streams
- playlist adds
- email signups
- social reach

### 2. coordinate creative assets

Share artwork, captions, and promotional assets so everyone posts consistent messaging.

### 3. map promotion timing

Schedule collaborative pushes around:
- pre-release teasers
- release day announcements
- follow-up posts with results

### 4. measure joint performance

Track specific outcomes from the collaboration:
- traffic from collaborator channels
- engagement lift
- new fans acquired

## Checklist

- [ ] collaboration goals aligned
- [ ] assets shared and approved
- [ ] promotion timing scheduled
- [ ] tracking established
- [ ] post-collaboration review planned

## Notes

- Keep collaborator communication clear and simple.
- Use the collaboration to introduce your music to a new but relevant audience.
