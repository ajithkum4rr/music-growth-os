# Phase 18 — Collaboration Outreach Guide

## Goal

Plan and execute outreach to creative collaborators with a clear value exchange.

## Why this matters

Good collaboration outreach leads to authentic creative partnerships and new audience access.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — define the collaboration offer

Write the value you bring and the value you want:
- production or music feature
- visual content or creative support
- audience cross-promotion

### Step 2 — identify the best collaborators

Choose people who:
- share your genre or vibe
- have a similar audience size or interest
- can add a creative or promotional boost

### Step 3 — write a clear outreach message

Include:
- why you admire their work
- what you want to create together
- the benefits for both of you
- an easy next step

### Step 4 — track outreach and responses

Record:
- contact date
- proposal details
- response status
- next steps

## What to record

- collaboration concept
- contact list
- outreach copy
- follow-up plan

## Common mistakes

- pitching vague collaborations.
- focusing only on follower counts.
- not explaining the mutual benefit.

## Notes

- Keep messages short and personalized.
- Offer a concrete concept rather than a broad idea.
