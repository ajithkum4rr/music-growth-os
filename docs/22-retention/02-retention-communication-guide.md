# Phase 22 — Retention Communication Guide

## Goal

Plan how to communicate with engaged listeners after the release.

## Why this matters

Good retention communication keeps fans engaged and builds longer-term loyalty.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — identify retention segments

Group fans by action:
- email subscribers
- recent listeners
- playlist savers
- social engagers

### Step 2 — choose the right message

For each group, choose a relevant communication:
- thank-you message
- behind-the-scenes update
- related music recommendation
- invitation to another action

### Step 3 — set timing

Schedule communications with a clear cadence:
- 1 week after release
- 2 weeks after release
- 4 weeks after release

### Step 4 — track engagement

Note:
- open or reply rates
- click-throughs
- repeat listens
- social interactions

## What to record

- audience segments
- message ideas
- send schedule
- engagement results

## Common mistakes

- sending the same message to every fan.
- overloading fans with too many emails.
- not tying the message to a clear next step.

## Notes

- Keep retention communication valuable and personal.
- Use it to deepen relationships, not just to promote.
