# Phase 22 — Fan Retention Plan

## Goal

Create a plan to keep engaged listeners coming back after the release.

## Why this matters

Retention turns one-time listeners into repeat fans and makes each release more valuable.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — identify engaged fans

Use your available data to spot:
- email subscribers
- social engagers
- playlist saves and followers

### Step 2 — create follow-up content

Plan content or emails for:
- thank-you notes
- behind-the-scenes updates
- next listen suggestions

### Step 3 — plan a retention sequence

Use a simple sequence:
- day 0: release thank-you or update
- day 3–5: share a story or recap
- day 7–10: offer an exclusive or next listen

### Step 4 — measure retention

Track:
- repeat listens
- email reopens
- returning visitors
- social re-engagement

## What to record

- retention content plan
- sequence schedule
- retention metrics

## Common mistakes

- Treating retention as an afterthought.
- Sending too much too soon.
- Not providing real value for returning fans.

## Notes

- Keep the follow-up personal and meaningful.
- Use retention to build loyalty, not just more promotion.
