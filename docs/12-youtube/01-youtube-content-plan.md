# Phase 12 — YouTube Content Plan

## Goal

Create a simple YouTube plan that showcases your music and grows your channel.

## Why this matters

YouTube is both a discovery channel and a long-term content asset for music releases.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — define your YouTube goal

Pick one objective for the first release cycle:
- video views and channel growth
- music discovery and playlist traffic
- fan engagement through story content

### Step 2 — choose formats

Start with 2–3 formats:
- release teaser or visualizer
- short behind-the-scenes clip
- lyric-style video
- channel trailer or story video

### Step 3 — optimize metadata

For each video:
- write a clear title
- include the release name and artist
- add a description with links
- use relevant tags

### Step 4 — publish schedule

Plan the first 2–3 uploads:
- release announcement video
- follow-up content after launch
- channel intro or artist story video

## What to record

- video ideas and formats.
- metadata plan.
- upload schedule.

## Common mistakes

- Publishing without link or CTA.
- Using the same video for every platform.
- Neglecting the video description.

## Notes

- Keep the first channel plan simple.
- Use YouTube shorts if you want faster reach.
