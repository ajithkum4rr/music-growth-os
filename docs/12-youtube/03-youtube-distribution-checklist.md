# Phase 12 — YouTube Distribution Checklist

## Goal

Make sure your YouTube content is distributed cleanly and reaches the right audience.

## Why this matters

A checklist prevents missed steps and keeps your YouTube release coordinated with the rest of the campaign.

## Distribution checklist

- [ ] title optimized for search and curiosity
- [ ] thumbnail is clear and compelling
- [ ] description includes release details, links, and CTAs
- [ ] target keywords and tags are chosen
- [ ] playlist placement on your channel is updated
- [ ] pinned comment includes streaming links and email signup
- [ ] end screens and cards are set up
- [ ] transcript and captions are uploaded
- [ ] community post is scheduled to support the video
- [ ] analytics tracking is enabled

## Notes

- Use the same branding and release messaging across YouTube and other channels.
- Update the checklist each time you publish a new video.
