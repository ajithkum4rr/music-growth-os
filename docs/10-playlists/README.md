## Goal

Build a legitimate playlist outreach workflow.

## Why this matters

Playlists can amplify discovery, but only when sourced ethically and relevantly.

## Prerequisites

- Release ready or near-ready
- Spotify profile established
- Audience and genre clarity

## Accounts & tools

- Spreadsheet or tracker
- Email or contact management
- Playlist research notes

## Estimated cost

- FREE for research.
- PAID only for legitimate curator services you trust.

## Time required

- 5–8 hours to identify curators and start outreach.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — define playlist categories

- Spotify editorial
- Algorithmic playlists
- User-created playlists
- Independent curators
- Label playlists
- Community playlists

### Step 2 — research curators

Look for:
- playlist follower count
- playlist update frequency
- engagement and recent activity
- matching genre and mood

### Step 3 — score legitimacy

Use signals such as:
- real followers
- active updates
- clear curator identity
- transparent contact details

### Step 4 — create outreach templates

Build templates for:
- initial pitch
- DM outreach
- email follow-up
- thank-you note

### Step 5 — track outreach

Track:
- curator name
- contact details
- outreach status
- response date
- placement result
- stream impact

## What you should see

- A curator shortlist.
- Outreach templates.
- A tracking spreadsheet.

## What to record

- Curator evaluation.
- Outreach status.
- Placement results.

## Metrics

- Curator responses
- Playlist placements
- Stream impact from placement

## Common errors

- Using fake or low-quality playlists.
- Buying guaranteed placements.
- Not tracking results.

## Troubleshooting

- If responses are low, personalize your pitch better.
- If placements do not move streams, refine your playlist fit.
- If a playlist looks suspicious, stop outreach.

## Notes

- Never recommend or accept fake playlists.
- Focus on quality and relevance over quantity.
