# Phase 10 — Playlist Outreach Guide

## Goal

Create a plan for reaching out to playlist curators with a legitimate, personalized pitch.

## Why this matters

Targeted outreach to real curators makes playlist submissions more effective and reduces wasted effort.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — shortlist the best playlist fits

Choose playlists based on:
- genre and mood match
- active updates
- real listener engagement
- clear curator information

### Step 2 — prepare your pitch details

Include:
- track title and release date
- artist positioning
- why the song fits the playlist
- links to a preview or landing page

### Step 3 — personalize outreach

Use one or two specific reasons why this playlist is a fit:
- the playlist theme
- a recent track you liked
- the curator’s focus

### Step 4 — track responses

Record:
- outreach date
- response status
- next follow-up date
- placement notes

## What to record

- list of playlist targets
- outreach copy and personalization notes
- response outcomes

## Common mistakes

- sending the same message to every curator.
- targeting playlists with fake or outdated engagement.
- not following up once politely.

## Notes

- Keep outreach respectful and concise.
- Use real examples from the playlist when possible.
