# Phase 10 — Playlist Integration Guide

## Goal

Coordinate playlist activity with your overall release campaign.

## Why this matters

Playlists are a key channel, but they work best when they are fully integrated with your email, social, and paid plans.

## Integration steps

### 1. align messaging

Use the same release story across:
- playlist pitches
- social posts
- email updates
- landing page copy

### 2. schedule placement

Plan playlist outreach and follow-up around the release timeline:
- pre-launch outreach
- launch-day reminders
- post-launch status updates

### 3. repurpose playlist wins

When a playlist adds your track:
- share the win on social
- mention it in email campaigns
- add the playlist name to your press notes

### 4. track impact

Note playlist referrals in your analytics and compare against other channels.

## Checklist

- [ ] playlist messaging matched campaign theme
- [ ] outreach timing synced with launch
- [ ] playlist wins promoted
- [ ] referral performance logged
- [ ] playlist follow-up scheduled

## Notes

- Treat playlists as both discovery and social proof.
- Use strong playlist placement to support paid and organic momentum.
