# Phase 10 — Playlist Research

## Goal

Research legitimate playlist opportunities and build a small outreach shortlist.

## Why this matters

Playlist outreach works best when you target playlists with real listeners and a good fit for your music.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — identify playlist types

- Editorial playlists
- User-generated playlists
- Curated playlists by genre or mood
- Local and community playlists

### Step 2 — evaluate quality

Look for:
- real follower counts and engagement
- consistent updates
- matching mood or genre
- clear curator contact information

### Step 3 — build a shortlist

Create a list of 10–15 playlists with:
- playlist name
- curator or owner
- follower count
- why it fits your release

### Step 4 — prepare outreach notes

Capture details for each playlist:
- email or contact method
- what makes your track a good fit
- any playlist rules

## What to record

- Playlist list and evaluation notes.
- Outreach priority.
- Fit rationale for each target.

## Common mistakes

- Chasing playlists with fake follower counts.
- Selecting playlists with the wrong mood.
- Sending generic outreach without personalization.

## Notes

- Focus on playlists you can reach with a personal note.
- Keep the outreach list manageable and real.
