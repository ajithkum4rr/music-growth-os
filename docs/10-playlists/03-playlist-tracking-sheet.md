# Phase 10 — Playlist Tracking Sheet

## Goal

Track playlist outreach status and follow-up actions for each target.

## Why this matters

A tracking sheet helps you stay organized and avoid repeating outreach mistakes.

## Fields to include

- Playlist name
- Curator / owner
- Playlist URL
- Contact method
- Outreach date
- Response status
- Follow-up date
- Notes / placement

## What to record

- outreach details
- responses and next steps
- placement information

## Common mistakes

- not updating status regularly
- losing track of who was contacted
- forgetting to follow up

## Notes

- Use this as a living document during your playlist outreach.
- Update it after every outreach and response.
