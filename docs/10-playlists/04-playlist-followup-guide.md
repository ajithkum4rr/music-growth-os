# Phase 10 — Playlist Follow-up Guide

## Goal

Create a consistent follow-up process for playlist outreach.

## Why this matters

A polite follow-up can increase your chances of getting a response without damaging relationships.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — wait before following up

Allow 5–7 business days after your first outreach before sending a follow-up.

### Step 2 — keep it short and friendly

Your message should:
- reference your first message
- remind the curator why the track fits their playlist
- offer a preview link again

### Step 3 — add value

Mention a small update or detail that makes the follow-up worth reading:
- a new press mention
- a related playlist placement
- a short release milestone

### Step 4 — track the outcome

Record:
- follow-up date
- response status
- next action

## What to record

- follow-up copy
- curator response
- any next steps

## Common mistakes

- following up too soon.
- sending the same message twice.
- making the follow-up too long.

## Notes

- Be professional and respectful.
- A single follow-up is usually enough.
