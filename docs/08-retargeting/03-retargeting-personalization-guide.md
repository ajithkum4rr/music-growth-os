# Phase 8 — Retargeting Personalization Guide

## Goal

Personalize retargeting messages to warm audiences based on their behavior.

## Why this matters

More relevant ads convert better and feel less repetitive.

## Audience signals

Use behavior to shape your message:
- visited landing page
- watched a preview clip
- signed up for email
- engaged with social content

## Message templates

### For email signups
- “Thanks for joining. Hear the song early.”

### For page visitors
- “Still curious? Stream the new track now.”

### For social engagers
- “You liked the teaser. Here’s the full release.”

### For engaged listeners
- “Fans are saving the song. Join them today.”

## Creative tips

- use the same visual style as the original touchpoint
- keep the CTA clear and simple
- highlight a benefit, not just the song title

## Checklist

- [ ] audience segments defined
- [ ] personalized messages written
- [ ] creative matched to each segment
- [ ] frequency limits set
- [ ] performance tracked by segment

## Notes

- Avoid showing the same ad too often.
- Use sequential messaging when possible.
