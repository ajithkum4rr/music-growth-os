## Goal

Build a funnel that moves people from cold interest to engaged fans.

## Why this matters

Retargeting turns early attention into higher-value action and stronger engagement.

## Prerequisites

- Meta ad setup
- Landing page or conversion destination
- Content and audience definitions

## Accounts & tools

- Meta Pixel or app event tracking
- Ads Manager
- Audience manager

## Estimated cost

- PAID for retargeting spend.
- Low cost to set up.

## Time required

- 3–6 hours to build the first funnel.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — define funnel stages

- Cold: people who have not interacted with you.
- Warm: people who viewed content or visited your landing page.
- Hot: people who engaged, clicked, or watched key videos.
- Fan: email subscribers, followers, or repeat listeners.

### Step 2 — map audience sources

- Video viewers
- Engagements
- Profile visitors
- Website/landing page visitors
- Link clickers
- Spotify listeners
- Email subscribers

### Step 3 — set retargeting windows

- 7 days for high-engagement audiences
- 14–30 days for broader warm audiences
- 30–60 days for fan-level campaigns

### Step 4 — choose creative for each stage

- Cold: discovery and curiosity.
- Warm: social proof and deeper story.
- Hot: release action and fan CTA.
- Fan: community and retention.

### Step 5 — exclude audiences

Avoid showing cold campaigns to warm or hot audiences.
Use exclusions to keep each stage focused.

## What you should see

- Defined cold/warm/hot/fan audiences.
- A retargeting campaign plan.
- Audience windows and exclusions.

## What to record

- Audience definitions.
- Retargeting rules.
- Creative mapping.

## Metrics

- Audience size
- Retargeting click-through rate
- Conversion rate
- Cost per retargeted action

## Common errors

- Retargeting the wrong audience.
- Using the same creative for every stage.
- Forgetting exclusions.

## Troubleshooting

- If the warm audience is too small, widen the source window.
- If the hot audience is expensive, test stronger ads or lower frequency.
- If fans see too many ads, increase exclusion settings.

## Notes

- Retargeting is most effective when audiences are clearly defined.
- If Meta tracking is not available, use manual warm audience rules.
