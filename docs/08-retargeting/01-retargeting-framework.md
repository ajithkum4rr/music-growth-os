# Phase 8 — Retargeting Framework

## Goal

Plan your retargeting audiences and creative rules for people who have already engaged.

## Why this matters

Retargeting helps move interested fans closer to action without needing a higher budget.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — define audience tiers

Set up three main audience buckets:
- cold: new people who have not seen your content
- warm: people who engaged with content or visited your page
- hot: people who took a high-intent action (email signup, link click)

### Step 2 — choose retargeting windows

Use simple time periods:
- 0–7 days for very warm audiences
- 7–14 days for warm retargeting
- 14–30 days for broader retargeting

### Step 3 — set exclusions

Prevent overlap by excluding:
- existing email subscribers
- people who already converted
- very recent engagers from cold campaigns

### Step 4 — map creative to audience

Plan creative that fits each stage:
- warm audience: behind-the-scenes or story content
- hot audience: direct release CTA or email sign-up reminder
- re-engagement: new hook or social proof

## What to record

- Audience definitions
- Window rules
- Exclusion logic
- Creative plan

## Common mistakes

- Retargeting everyone with the same message.
- Using too broad a window.
- Not excluding people who already converted.

## Notes

- Keep the first retargeting setup simple.
- Use data from the first week to refine windows and messaging.
