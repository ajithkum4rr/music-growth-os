# Phase 16 — PR Email Template

## Goal

Create a simple, personalized PR outreach email for your release.

## Why this matters

A direct, relevant email increases response rates and helps your pitch stand out.

## Template

Subject: New release from `ajkay` — [Release title] out [Release date]

Hello [Name],

I’m `ajkay`, and I’m releasing a new track called **[Release title]** on **[Release date]**. The song is [short angle: mood, story, or sound], and it would be a strong fit for [publication/audience] because [reason].

Key details:
- Artist: `ajkay`
- Track: [Release title]
- Release date: [Release date]
- Why it matters: [one short sentence]

I’d love to send you a preview and learn if this could work for [publication/show/channel].

Thanks for your time,

[Your name]
[Your email]
[Link to landing page or artist press kit]

## Notes

- Personalize the first sentence to the recipient or outlet.
- Keep the email short and easy to scan.
- Offer a preview and make it simple to reply.
