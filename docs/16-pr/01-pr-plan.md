# Phase 16 — PR Plan

## Goal

Create a press outreach plan that highlights your release story and reaches relevant music media.

## Why this matters

PR builds credibility and can drive valuable referral traffic when done with clear targeting and a good pitch.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — identify press targets

Choose outlets that match your release:
- music blogs
- local and niche publications
- podcasts
- playlist blogs

### Step 2 — define your story angles

Pick 2–3 strong angles such as:
- the release theme
- your artist journey
- a unique production or collaboration story

### Step 3 — build a target list

For each target note:
- outlet name
- editor or writer
- contact method
- why the release fits

### Step 4 — draft outreach copy

Write a concise pitch with:
- a compelling subject line
- one short release summary
- the story angle
- release date and link

## What to record

- Media target list
- Pitch copy
- Response tracking

## Common mistakes

- Sending generic pitches.
- Targeting outlets that don’t fit your genre.
- Following up too soon or too late.

## Notes

- Prioritize relevance over volume.
- Respect each journalist’s preferred outreach channel.
