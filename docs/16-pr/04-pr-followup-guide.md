# Phase 16 — PR Follow-up Guide

## Goal

Keep your PR outreach moving with a polite follow-up process.

## Why this matters

A follow-up can significantly improve response rates without being pushy.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — wait the right amount of time

Allow 5–7 business days before sending a follow-up. This gives editors time to review your first email.

### Step 2 — keep it short

Use a brief message:
- reference your first email
- remind them of the release and why it fits
- offer a quick preview or link

### Step 3 — provide an easy next step

Suggest a simple action such as:
- replying if they want a preview
- letting you know if they need more info
- confirming the best time to follow up again

### Step 4 — log the follow-up

Track:
- follow-up date
- response status
- outcome or next step

## What to record

- email copy
- follow-up timing
- responses received

## Common mistakes

- following up too soon.
- writing a long message.
- sounding desperate or overly informal.

## Notes

- Use a friendly tone.
- Keep follow-ups professional and concise.
