# Phase 16 — PR Target List

## Goal

Create a focused list of press and media targets for your release.

## Why this matters

A strong target list ensures your outreach goes to the right outlets, not generic contacts.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — decide your coverage goals

Choose the type of coverage you want:
- music blogs
- local press
- podcast interviews
- editorial playlists

### Step 2 — research targets

For each target, capture:
- outlet name
- editor / writer / host
- contact method
- audience fit
- why your release matches

### Step 3 — rank the opportunities

Use a simple scorecard with:
- relevance
- likelihood of response
- publication authority
- timing fit

### Step 4 — note personalization points

Record what you can personalize for each outreach:
- recent coverage
- editorial focus
- audience interest

## What to record

- target list with contact details
- why each outlet is a fit
- outreach priority order
- follow-up timing

## Common mistakes

- targeting only large outlets.
- sending generic outreach.
- missing the publication’s submission guidelines.

## Notes

- Keep the list manageable.
- Focus on quality and relevance over quantity.
