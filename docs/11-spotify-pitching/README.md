## Goal

Prepare a professional Spotify editorial pitch.

## Why this matters

A strong pitch increases the chance of editorial and algorithmic playlist consideration.

## Prerequisites

- Release metadata ready
- Spotify for Artists access
- Release story and artist positioning

## Accounts & tools

- Spotify for Artists dashboard
- Pitch notes and story document

## Estimated cost

- FREE.

## Time required

- 2–4 hours to prepare and submit.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — gather pitch inputs

Collect:
- song title
- artist name
- release date
- genre
- mood
- story and inspiration

### Step 2 — write release notes

Include:
- what makes the song unique
- why listeners will connect
- any relevant achievements or context
- how the track fits listeners’ moods

### Step 3 — submit in Spotify for Artists

Use the submission form in the release section.
Provide clear, concise notes and avoid filler.

### Step 4 — track the submission

Record the date submitted and any follow-up notes.

## What you should see

- A completed pitch submission.
- A concise release note document.
- A tracking entry for the pitch.

## What to record

- Pitch text.
- Submission date.
- Any feedback or playlist notices.

## Metrics

- Pitch submitted
- Editorial consideration (if visible)
- Playlist adds from editorial

## Common errors

- Writing generic pitch copy.
- Ignoring the release story.
- Missing the Spotify submission deadline.

## Troubleshooting

- If the form is unavailable, verify your release and distributor status.
- If you’re unsure of the angle, ask yourself why this track matters now.

## Notes

- Spotify editorial pitching is one part of a broader playlist strategy.
- Keep the pitch honest and listener-focused.
