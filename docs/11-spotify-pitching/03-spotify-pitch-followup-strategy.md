# Phase 11 — Spotify Pitch Follow-up Strategy

## Goal

Turn pitch outreach into action by following up with a structured and respectful process.

## Why this matters

A single outreach usually gets lost. Following up increases your chances of getting heard and shows professionalism.

## Follow-up workflow

### 1. wait the right amount of time

Allow 3–5 business days after your initial pitch before following up.

### 2. add value in the follow-up

Reference your previous message and include a new insight:
- updated artist story
- new social momentum
- an angle tailored for editorial playlists

### 3. keep it short

A follow-up should be a quick reminder, not a repeat of the full pitch.

### 4. track responses

Log whether the curator replied, expressed interest, requested more assets, or declined.

### 5. limit follow-ups

Send a maximum of two follow-ups before moving on.

## Follow-up template outline

- quick greeting
- reminder of the track and release date
- one new supporting note
- link to the music and pitch assets

## Checklist

- [ ] first follow-up scheduled
- [ ] second follow-up planned
- [ ] message personalized for each contact
- [ ] response status tracked
- [ ] next action recorded

## Notes

- Respect curator time and avoid pressure.
- Use follow-up outcomes to improve future pitches.
