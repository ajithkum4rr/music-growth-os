# Phase 11 — Spotify Pitch Template

## Goal

Write a clear and concise Spotify editorial pitch for your release.

## Template

**Track:** [Song title]

**Release date:** [Release date]

**Artist:** `ajkay`

**Genre / mood:** [Genre and mood]

**Why it matters:**
[Short statement about the song’s story, sound, or emotional hook. Keep it specific and compelling.]

**What makes it a fit:**
- [Reason 1: e.g. ideal for late-night playlists]
- [Reason 2: e.g. cinematic mood and emotional vocals]
- [Reason 3: e.g. strong artist story or local angle]

**Additional notes:**
- [Any production, collaboration, or release detail that adds context]

## Notes

- Keep the pitch under 250 words.
- Use your strongest angle first.
- Be honest and avoid exaggeration.
