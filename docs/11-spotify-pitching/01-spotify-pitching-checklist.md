# Phase 11 — Spotify Pitching Checklist

## Goal

Prepare your Spotify editorial pitch and landing materials for release submission.

## Why this matters

A strong, concise pitch increases the odds of editorial consideration and supporting placements.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — collect pitch details

- Song title
- Release date
- Genre and mood
- Artist story / release story

### Step 2 — write your pitch narrative

Answer:
- What is this song about?
- Why does it matter now?
- What makes it unique?

### Step 3 — choose a release category

Match the song to the most relevant Spotify editorial category:
- mood
- genre
- activity
- local scene

### Step 4 — submit in Spotify for Artists

- Use the pitch form before the release deadline.
- Keep the pitch under 250 words.
- Include the most compelling angle first.

## What to record

- Pitch copy.
- Selected playlist categories.
- Submission date.

## Common mistakes

- Writing a pitch that is too vague.
- Missing the submission window.
- Using too much jargon.

## Notes

- Keep the pitch honest and human.
- Submit early to give editorial teams time to review.
