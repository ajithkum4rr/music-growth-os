# Phase 0 — Brand Setup

## Goal

Define the core identity, positioning, and creative direction for `ajkay`.

## Why this matters

Brand clarity helps you create consistent content, attract the right listeners, and communicate value.
People follow artists whose identity feels coherent and memorable.

## What this includes

- Artist story and values
- Genre and mood
- Target listener profile
- Visual direction and tone of voice

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — write your positioning statement

Answer these questions:
- Who is `ajkay`?
- What kind of music do you make?
- Why should someone listen?
- What feeling do you want listeners to take away?

Write one short paragraph that covers all four.

### Step 2 — define your genre and mood

Choose the words people will use to describe your music.
Examples:
- Electronic pop with emotional vocals
- Chill house for late-night listeners
- Futuristic indie with cinematic soundscapes

### Step 3 — build your artist profile

Create a simple profile in notes with:
- Artist name: `ajkay`
- Tagline: one sentence that sums up your sound
- Core themes: 3–5 words or phrases
- Your story: what motivates you, what your sound stands for

### Step 4 — choose content directions

List 4–6 content themes that fit your brand, such as:
- Studio process
- Song stories
- Visual storytelling
- Production tips
- Emotional or personal context

### Step 5 — document visual style

Decide on:
- Color palette or mood
- Typography or font style (for simple text-based assets)
- Imagery style: moody, bright, cinematic, minimal
- Video style: performance, vibey, narrative, lyric visuals

## What to record

- Positioning statement
- Genre and mood keywords
- Content theme list
- Visual direction notes
- Example artists and reference links

## Common mistakes

- Making the brand too broad.
- Using different artist identities across platforms.
- Starting with a weak or unclear tagline.
- Ignoring the listener’s perspective.

## Troubleshooting

- If the positioning feels vague, simplify to one sentence.
- If you can’t pick one mood, choose the strongest two and keep them consistent.
- If you feel stuck, review 3 artists you admire and write down why they feel cohesive.

## Security

- This is creative work. Keep it in your notes and version-controlled repo.

## Notes

- Your brand can evolve. This is the first iteration, not the final version.
- Be honest and specific rather than generic.

## Advanced notes

- Later, use this brand foundation to create pitch copy, ad copy, and PR angles.
- Keep the same language across your bio, captions, and release notes.
