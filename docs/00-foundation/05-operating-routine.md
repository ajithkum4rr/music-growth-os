# Phase 0 — Operating Routine

## Goal

Define a realistic weekly operating routine for a solo artist.

## Why this matters

A routine turns strategy into repeatable progress.

## Example weekly schedule

- Monday: analytics and review
- Tuesday: content planning and creation
- Wednesday: audience research and outreach
- Thursday: campaign optimization and ads
- Friday: fan engagement and retention
- Weekend: creative work and release preparation

## Notes

- Adjust based on your availability.
- Keep one day open for catch-up.
- Review progress weekly.
