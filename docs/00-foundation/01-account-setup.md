# Phase 0 — Account Setup

## Goal

Create the core accounts and secure them for your first release.

## Why this matters

Account readiness is the base layer for every later phase: ads, analytics, email, and distribution.
If these accounts are unstable or insecure, your launch will be harder and riskier.

## Accounts to set up now

1. Spotify for Artists
2. Meta Business Manager
3. Facebook Page
4. Instagram account
5. YouTube channel
6. TikTok account
7. Email service provider
8. Landing page or link-in-bio tool
9. Google Analytics / GA4 (optional but recommended)

## What each account is

- Spotify for Artists: your official music profile and analytics.
- Meta Business Manager: central manager for Facebook, Instagram, ads, and assets.
- Facebook Page: professional presence for social credibility.
- Instagram account: content and fan engagement hub.
- YouTube channel: music video and search discovery channel.
- TikTok account: short-form audience discovery engine.
- Email provider: direct fan communication channel.
- Landing page: a single destination for links and opt-ins.
- Google Analytics / GA4: website traffic and referral tracking.

## Step-by-step setup

### Spotify for Artists

1. Go to `artists.spotify.com`.
2. Sign in with the Spotify account you want to use.
3. Request access to your artist profile.
4. Verify ownership via your distributor or label.
5. Save your login and URL separately.

### Meta Business Manager

1. Go to `business.facebook.com`.
2. Create a Business Manager account for `ajkay`.
3. Add your business details and verify your email.
4. Create or connect a Facebook Page.
5. Create or connect an Instagram account.
6. Add a payment method if you plan to advertise.
7. Enable 2FA on the primary user account.

### Facebook Page

1. Create a Facebook Page in Business Manager.
2. Use `ajkay` as the Page name.
3. Add profile and cover images.
4. Write a short, clear bio.
5. Save the Page URL.

### Instagram account

1. Create an Instagram account for `ajkay`.
2. Use a consistent username.
3. Switch to a creator account if possible.
4. Add a profile photo and bio.
5. Connect it to the Facebook Page.

### YouTube channel

1. Sign in to YouTube with a Google account.
2. Create a channel name matching `ajkay`.
3. Add channel art and description.
4. Fill in contact details and links.

### TikTok account

1. Download TikTok or use the web signup.
2. Create a creator account for `ajkay`.
3. Add a profile picture and bio.
4. Link your Instagram and/or YouTube if available.

### Email service provider

1. Choose a free plan: Mailchimp, ConvertKit, or similar.
2. Create an account with your email.
3. Set up a list or audience.
4. Create a simple signup form.
5. Prepare a basic welcome email.

### Landing page tool

1. Choose a simple tool: Carrd, Linktree, or a small website builder.
2. Create a page that shows your music, links, and email signup.
3. Add Spotify and social links.
4. Add a short artist blurb.

### Google Analytics / GA4

1. Create a Google Analytics account if you have a website.
2. Get the tracking ID.
3. Add it to your landing page.
4. Note that this is optional for early MVP.

## Security checklist

- [ ] Use unique passwords.
- [ ] Enable 2FA where available.
- [ ] Store passwords in a password manager.
- [ ] Do not store passwords in repo files.
- [ ] Keep account recovery details updated.

## Common mistakes

- Creating a business account with personal email only.
- Skipping 2FA.
- Using inconsistent artist names.
- Mixing personal and artist accounts.

## What to record

- Account URLs
- Profile URLs
- Primary login email
- Notes on any verification steps

## Verification notes

- `VERIFY CURRENT` platform signup flows.
