# Phase 20 — Weekly Review Guide

## Goal

Create a consistent weekly review process for your music campaign analytics.

## Why this matters

Weekly reviews help you spot trends early and make faster, smarter adjustments.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — gather the week’s metrics

Collect:
- traffic or landing page visits
- email signups
- streams and saves
- social engagement
- ad performance

### Step 2 — compare to the previous week

Ask:
- what increased?
- what decreased?
- did any channel perform differently than expected?

### Step 3 — note one key insight

Write a short insight:
- the most useful thing you learned
- what changed and why it matters

### Step 4 — decide one action

Choose one small action to improve the next week:
- refresh a creative
- adjust a CTA
- tighten an audience
- send a follow-up email

## What to record

- weekly metric summary
- insight statement
- next action

## Common mistakes

- reviewing too late.
- focusing on too many metrics.
- skipping the action step.

## Notes

- Keep the review short and actionable.
- Use it as a weekly habit in your operating routine.
