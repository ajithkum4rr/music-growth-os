# Phase 20 — Data Validation Guide

## Goal

Confirm that your analytics data is accurate and meaningful.

## Why this matters

Bad data can lead to bad decisions. Validation ensures you trust the numbers you use to optimize the campaign.

## Validation steps

### 1. verify tracking installation

Check that all tracking scripts and pixels are present on the landing page and relevant pages.

### 2. test event capture

Trigger core events manually and verify they appear in your analytics dashboards.

### 3. compare sources

Ensure referral data matches across tools:
- landing page analytics
- email platform traffic
- ad reporting

### 4. audit UTM consistency

Verify UTM parameters are applied consistently across all campaign links.

### 5. review data delays

Know how often reports refresh and factor delays into your daily review.

## Checklist

- [ ] tracking installed
- [ ] events tested
- [ ] source data compared
- [ ] UTM parameters audited
- [ ] refresh cadence confirmed

## Notes

- Validate data before making campaign changes.
- Repeat validation after major updates to tracking or landing pages.
