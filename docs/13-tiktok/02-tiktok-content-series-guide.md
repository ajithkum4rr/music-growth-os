# Phase 13 — TikTok Content Series Guide

## Goal

Plan a small series of TikTok posts that build momentum around your release.

## Why this matters

A consistent series helps new viewers recognize your style and keeps your content focused.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — choose a theme for the series

Examples:
- release countdown
- song story in parts
- production breakdown
- fan reaction series

### Step 2 — define 3–5 posts

For each post, plan:
- hook
- visual idea
- sound or trend use
- CTA

### Step 3 — keep the series connected

Use a consistent hashtag, visual style, or text frame to link the series.

### Step 4 — review performance

Look for:
- which hooks resonated
- how viewers engaged
- which videos drove profile visits

## What to record

- series concept
- post ideas
- performance notes

## Common mistakes

- changing themes too quickly.
- posting without a clear CTA.
- not iterating from early results.

## Notes

- Keep the series small and manageable.
- A single strong theme is better than too many unrelated ideas.
