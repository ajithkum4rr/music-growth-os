# Phase 13 — TikTok Test Plan

## Goal

Plan your first TikTok content tests around the release and audience hooks.

## Why this matters

TikTok performance depends on strong hooks, repeatable formats, and fast learning.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — choose your core hook

Pick one strong idea for the first test:
- a vocal or production moment
- a mood or story hook
- a behind-the-scenes reveal

### Step 2 — decide the format

Select 1–2 formats:
- music-led clip
- talking head story
- text overlay narration
- visual mood montage

### Step 3 — post and track

Publish the first 2–3 videos and note:
- time and date
- hook description
- outcome (views, likes, comments)

### Step 4 — iterate quickly

After each post, ask:
- Did the hook land?
- Was the CTA clear?
- What should change next?

## What to record

- Hook idea.
- Video format.
- Performance notes.

## Common mistakes

- Testing too many ideas at once.
- Skipping the first meaningful CTA.
- Ignoring audience feedback.

## Notes

- Use the same sound or concept across a small series.
- Keep the first test low pressure and learning-focused.
