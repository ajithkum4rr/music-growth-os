# Phase 4 — Content Production Workflow

## Goal

Create a repeatable process for producing release-related content.

## Why this matters

A predictable workflow reduces friction and helps you ship content consistently.

## Workflow steps

### 1. plan the content pillars

Choose 3–4 content themes that support the release:
- release story
- behind the song
- fan experience
- performance highlights

### 2. map content formats

Use formats that work for your audience:
- short video clips
- static social posts
- email newsletter copy
- blog or press notes

### 3. batch creation

Produce content in blocks to stay efficient:
- write captions and scripts
- shoot or record assets
- edit and finalize visuals

### 4. schedule distribution

Place content on a calendar with clear publish dates.
Use one content calendar for all channels.

### 5. review and iterate

After publishing, note what performed best.
Update the workflow with what worked.

## Checklist

- [ ] content pillars defined
- [ ] creative brief written
- [ ] assets produced in batches
- [ ] copy and captions drafted
- [ ] publish calendar created
- [ ] quality check completed
- [ ] performance review planned

## Notes

- Keep each asset aligned to one clear objective.
- Reuse strong content across platforms with small edits.
