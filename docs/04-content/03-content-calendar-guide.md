# Phase 4 — Content Calendar Guide

## Goal

Plan and schedule content consistently across your release campaign.

## Why this matters

A content calendar keeps your ideas organized and prevents last-minute publishing gaps.

## Steps

### 1. map key campaign moments

Identify the dates that deserve content:
- release announcement
- preview drops
- playlist and press wins
- follow-up engagement posts

### 2. choose formats

Pick content formats for each moment:
- short videos
- static graphics
- audio teasers
- email updates

### 3. assign channels

Decide where each item will publish:
- Instagram
- TikTok
- YouTube
- email
- blog

### 4. schedule publication

Set publish dates and times in a single calendar.

### 5. review and adjust

Update the calendar with actual publish status and performance notes.

## Checklist

- [ ] campaign moments mapped
- [ ] formats chosen
- [ ] channels assigned
- [ ] schedule created
- [ ] performance notes planned

## Notes

- Keep the calendar as simple as possible.
- Use the same content plan to drive email, socials, and landing page traffic.
