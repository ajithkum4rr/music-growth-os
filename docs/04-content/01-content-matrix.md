# Phase 4 — Content Matrix

## Goal

Build a content matrix that ties release assets, audience moments, and platform formats together.

## Why this matters

Good content planning keeps your posts purposeful and aligned with your release story. It also reduces ad hoc posting and improves consistency.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — list core content themes

Choose 3–5 themes that support your release, such as:
- Song story
- Behind the scenes
- Visual mood
- Fan moments
- Production process

### Step 2 — match formats by platform

Create a table or list for each platform:
- Instagram: carousel, Reels, stories
- TikTok: short clips, sound-first hooks, trends
- YouTube: lyric preview, visualizer, short form
- Email: release story, update, newsletter

### Step 3 — assign timing

For each content idea, note:
- When to publish (pre-release, release week, post-release)
- Primary CTA (listen, follow, sign up)
- Asset type needed (video, image, caption)

### Step 4 — capture reuse paths

Identify how one asset can become multiple posts:
- Turn a lyric video into a TikTok clip
- Turn cover art into a social graphic
- Turn a song story into an email note

## What to record

- Content theme list
- Platform format map
- Post timing and CTAs
- Asset reuse ideas

## Common mistakes

- Planning only one platform.
- Creating content without a CTA.
- Mixing too many unrelated content ideas.

## Notes

- Keep the first content plan simple and repeatable.
- Focus on quality over quantity for early releases.
