# Phase 15 — Email Sequence Guide

## Goal

Create a simple email sequence to welcome new subscribers and promote your release.

## Why this matters

A thoughtful email sequence helps turn signups into engaged fans and keeps your message consistent.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — define your sequence goals

Decide what you want subscribers to do:
- listen to the release
- follow you on streaming platforms
- stay engaged with new content

### Step 2 — choose the sequence length

Start with 3–4 emails:
- welcome email
- release announcement
- next story or behind-the-scenes
- follow-up / reminder

### Step 3 — write the main emails

Email ideas:
- welcome: thank them and introduce your music
- release: share the new track and where to listen
- story: explain the meaning or making of the song
- reminder: invite them to save, share, or follow

### Step 4 — schedule the send dates

Keep timing simple:
- day 0: welcome
- day 3–5: release announcement or story
- day 7–10: follow-up or next action

### Step 5 — track performance

Monitor:
- open rate
- click rate
- signups from links
- replies or engagement

## What to record

- email goals
- send schedule
- key messages
- performance notes

## Common mistakes

- sending too much too soon.
- asking for action without a clear reason.
- using overly generic copy.

## Notes

- Use the same brand language as your other marketing.
- Keep each email short, clear, and valuable.
