# Phase 15 — Email Capture Guide

## Goal

Set up email capture and a basic welcome flow to convert fans into a direct audience.

## Why this matters

Email is a direct channel you own. It creates a reliable way to communicate with fans beyond social algorithms.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — choose an email provider

Use a simple provider like:
- Mailchimp
- ConvertKit
- MailerLite

### Step 2 — create a signup form

Add a form to your landing page or link it directly.
Collect only essentials:
- email address
- first name (optional)

### Step 3 — build a welcome email

Write one welcome message that:
- thanks the subscriber
- introduces your music
- includes a link to your release or social profile

### Step 4 — set up tracking

Track:
- signups per day
- form conversion rate
- email open and click rates

## What to record

- Email provider account
- Form URL
- Welcome email copy
- subscriber tracking notes

## Common mistakes

- Asking for too much information.
- Delaying the welcome email.
- Sending email only once and then forgetting the list.

## Notes

- Keep the email simple and brand-aligned.
- Use email to share release updates and exclusive content.
