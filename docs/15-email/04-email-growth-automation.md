# Phase 15 — Email Growth Automation

## Goal

Automate email growth activities without losing the personal connection.

## Why this matters

Automation can scale your list-building while preserving the signal that drives new fans.

## Key automation workflows

### 1. welcome sequence

Send an automated welcome email to new subscribers with:
- a thank-you message
- release preview or best track
- social and playlist links

### 2. onboarding path

For new subscribers, deliver a short drip sequence that:
- introduces your artist story
- shares your latest release
- invites engagement

### 3. reminder triggers

Set up behavior-based triggers for:
- abandoned signup flows
- missed release launches
- re-engagement of dormant subscribers

### 4. list growth campaigns

Use automation to feed new leads from:
- landing page signups
- social contests
- playlist and blog partnerships

## Checklist

- [ ] welcome sequence defined
- [ ] onboarding path created
- [ ] trigger rules documented
- [ ] content mapped to automation steps
- [ ] performance measured and adjusted

## Notes

- Keep the automation helpful and not too frequent.
- Personalize where possible with fan-first language.
