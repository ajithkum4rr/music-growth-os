# Phase 15 — Email Template Library

## Goal

Build a simple collection of email templates for your release campaign.

## Why this matters

Having ready templates speeds up communication and keeps messaging consistent.

## Templates to include

- Welcome email
- Release announcement
- Story / behind-the-release
- Reminder / follow-up
- Re-engagement message

## Common mistakes

- using the same subject line for every email.
- keeping templates too generic.
- not adapting the tone to the audience.
