## Goal

Set up a legal email list and a fan capture workflow.

## Why this matters

Email is the most direct channel for reliable fan communication.

## Prerequisites

- Email provider account
- Landing page or signup form
- Release plan

## Accounts & tools

- Mailchimp, ConvertKit, or similar
- Signup form builder
- Email template editor

## Estimated cost

- FREE to start.
- PAID as subscriber volume grows.

## Time required

- 3–5 hours to set up and draft the first emails.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — choose an email provider

Pick one with nice forms and good deliverability.

### Step 2 — create a signup form

Add it to your landing page and social links.

### Step 3 — create a welcome email

Deliver:
- a thank-you message
- the release link or exclusive content
- what to expect next

### Step 4 — create a release email

Draft an email for launch day and schedule it.

### Step 5 — keep consent clear

- Add unsubscribe links.
- Use clear opt-in language.
- Respect privacy.

## What you should see

- A working email list.
- A welcome message.
- A release announcement draft.

## What to record

- Email provider details.
- Signup sources.
- Email performance.

## Metrics

- Subscribers
- Open rate
- Click rate
- Unsubscribe rate

## Common errors

- Not using clear consent language.
- Sending emails without testing.
- Not tracking email performance.

## Troubleshooting

- If open rates are low, improve the subject line.
- If clicks are low, clarify the CTA.
- If unsubscribes rise, reduce frequency or improve relevance.

## Notes

- Email should be a value channel, not spam.
- Focus on fans who want to hear from you.
