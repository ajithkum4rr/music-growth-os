## Goal

Build a safe and structured Meta ads operating procedure.

## Why this matters

Meta ads let you scale discovery, test audiences, and drive traffic when done correctly.

## Prerequisites

- Meta Business Manager
- Facebook Page and Instagram account
- Landing page or music destination
- Some content assets

## Accounts & tools

- Meta Business Manager
- Ads Manager
- Events Manager
- Meta Pixel (optional for later)
- Payment method

## Estimated cost

- PAID only for ad spend.
- Start small to minimize risk.

## Time required

- 4–8 hours for setup and the first campaign.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — understand the tools

- Business Manager: account and asset management.
- Ads Manager: campaign creation and reporting.
- Business Suite: page management and inbox.
- Events Manager: tracking conversions.
- Instagram Boost: quick promotion of a single post (OPTIONAL).

### Step 2 — set up the account

- Create a Business Manager.
- Add your Page and Instagram account.
- Set up the ad account.
- Add a payment method.
- Enable 2FA on the admin account.

### Step 3 — define naming conventions

Use clear names for:
- Campaigns
- Ad sets
- Ads
- UTMs

### Step 4 — choose your first objective

Start with awareness or traffic:
- Reach or brand awareness for cold audiences.
- Traffic or engagement for landing page visitors.

### Step 5 — create your first test

Test one audience, one creative, and one CTA.
Use a small budget and look for early signals.

## What you should see

- A working Meta ad account.
- A first campaign draft.
- Clear naming and tracking.

## What to record

- Campaign structure.
- Budget and objective.
- Initial performance metrics.

## Metrics

- Impressions
- CPM
- CPC
- CTR
- Landing page clicks
- Cost per action

## Common errors

- Starting with a large budget.
- Rushing naming conventions.
- Using too many audiences and creatives at once.

## Troubleshooting

- If ads are rejected, check Meta policy and creative guidelines.
- If the account is restricted, review account setup and payment info.
- If performance is poor, pause and test a new creative.

## Notes

- Meta ads are a tool, not a guarantee. Start with learning.
- Keep the first campaigns simple and measurable.
