# Phase 7 — Meta Ads Budget Guide

## Goal

Plan a realistic initial budget for your first Meta ad tests.

## Why this matters

A clear budget plan prevents overspending and helps you learn quickly from a small test.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — choose a test budget

Start small and manageable:
- 10–20 USD per day for the first test
- 50–100 USD total for the first campaign

### Step 2 — divide the budget

Allocate across the funnel:
- 50% cold audience testing
- 30% warm retargeting
- 20% brand or awareness support

### Step 3 — set duration

Run the test for 5–7 days to gather enough data.

### Step 4 — define success thresholds

Track simple metrics:
- CPM
- CTR
- CPC
- conversion cost

### Step 5 — review before scaling

If the test meets your target metrics, consider a small increase.
If not, refine creative or audience before spending more.

## What to record

- budget amount
- daily spend plan
- audience budget split
- performance thresholds

## Common mistakes

- starting with too large a budget.
- changing the test too early.
- testing too many audiences or creatives at once.

## Notes

- Keep the first Meta test lean and focused.
- Use the budget guide as a learning tool, not a fixed rule.
