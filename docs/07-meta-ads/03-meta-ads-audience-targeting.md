# Phase 7 — Meta Ads Audience Targeting

## Goal

Choose the right audiences for your first Meta ad test.

## Why this matters

Good targeting improves learnings and reduces wasted spend.

## Targeting approach

### 1. start with broad audience signals

Use interests that match your genre, mood, or fan communities.

### 2. create tight custom audiences

Build audiences from:
- landing page visitors
- email subscribers
- social engagers

### 3. test lookalikes carefully

Use lookalike audiences based on your best fans or email list.

### 4. avoid audience overlap

Keep each test audience distinct so results are easier to interpret.

### 5. review early performance

Pause audiences that underperform and reallocate spend to winners.

## Checklist

- [ ] broad interest audience defined
- [ ] custom audiences created
- [ ] lookalike audiences configured
- [ ] overlap checked
- [ ] early performance review planned

## Notes

- Use audience targeting to validate the right message, not just to chase impressions.
- Keep the first test narrow enough to learn quickly.
