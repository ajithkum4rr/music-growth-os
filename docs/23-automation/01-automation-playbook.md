# Phase 23 — Automation Playbook

## Goal

Identify safe automation opportunities that save time without reducing control.

## Why this matters

Automation can free up time for creative work, but it should never replace human approval on key marketing actions.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — list repeatable tasks

Record tasks you do regularly, such as:
- weekly analytics notes
- campaign status check-ins
- social post scheduling reminders
- email sign-up reports

### Step 2 — choose safe automation candidates

Pick tasks that are low-risk and repeatable:
- reminders and checklists
- simple reporting exports
- content publishing reminders

### Step 3 — define human checks

For each automation, note what must stay manual:
- final ad launch decisions
- creative approval
- budget changes

### Step 4 — document the automation rules

Write a simple rule for each task:
- what triggers it
- what it does
- who approves it

## What to record

- repeatable task list
- automation candidate notes
- manual approval points

## Common mistakes

- automating strategy or creative decisions.
- automating without clear approvals.
- relying on automation for urgent tasks.

## Notes

- Keep automation supportive, not controlling.
- Start with one or two safe automations.
