# Phase 19 — Launch Day Checklist

## Goal

Make launch day predictable and manageable with a simple checklist.

## Why this matters

Launch day is the most active moment in the campaign. A checklist helps you stay focused and avoid last-minute issues.

## Launch day checklist

- [ ] landing page is live and functioning
- [ ] email announcement has been sent
- [ ] social posts are scheduled or published
- [ ] ad campaigns are active
- [ ] tracking pixels and UTM links are verified
- [ ] streaming links and assets are live
- [ ] press and playlist follow-up messages sent
- [ ] team and collaborators have launch day roles
- [ ] monitoring dashboard is open
- [ ] surge response plan is ready

## Notes

- Keep the launch day checklist visible to everyone involved.
- Prioritize the tasks that directly impact discovery and fan action.
