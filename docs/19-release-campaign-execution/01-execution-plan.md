# Phase 19 — Release Campaign Execution Plan

## Goal

Create a clear execution plan for your release launch and the first weeks after.

## Why this matters

This phase connects everything: content, ads, outreach, email, and timing. A solid execution plan keeps launch activity aligned and accountable.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — lock in the key dates

- Release date
- Landing page live date
- Email announcement date
- Ad start date
- Post-release review date

### Step 2 — align channel activities

Map the main actions for each channel:
- organic socials
- Meta ads
- email
- PR / outreach
- Spotify pitching

### Step 3 — assign responsibilities

Record who owns each task and when it is due.
For a solo artist, this may be you plus any collaborators.

### Step 4 — create a daily check-in plan

Define the first 7–10 days of launch activity:
- day 0: release announcement
- day 1–3: support posts + engagement
- day 4–7: optimization and follow-up

## What to record

- Campaign timeline
- Channel checklist
- Daily launch tasks
- Post-launch review actions

## Common mistakes

- Launching without a follow-up plan.
- Not coordinating content and paid activity.
- Missing the first week of review.

## Notes

- Keep the campaign plan practical and action-focused.
- Use this plan to stay on top of launch momentum.
