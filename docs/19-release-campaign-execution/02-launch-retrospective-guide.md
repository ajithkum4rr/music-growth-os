# Phase 19 — Launch Retrospective Guide

## Goal

Review the launch and capture practical takeaways for the next campaign.

## Why this matters

A fast retrospective turns launch momentum into continuous improvement and helps avoid repeating the same mistakes.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — gather launch results

Collect the key outcomes:
- streams and listeners
- landing page visits
- email signups
- social engagement
- campaign spend and performance

### Step 2 — compare to the original plan

Review what went as expected and what changed:
- which channels overperformed
- which tasks slipped
- any unexpected wins or issues

### Step 3 — document lessons learned

Answer:
- what worked best?
- what was confusing or ineffective?
- what should change next time?

### Step 4 — assign improvements

List 2–3 specific actions for the next release:
- timing changes
- creative updates
- better tracking

## What to record

- launch summary
- performance highlights
- lessons learned
- next-release improvements

## Common mistakes

- skipping the review and moving on too quickly.
- focusing only on metrics without context.
- not updating the playbook with real lessons.

## Notes

- Keep the retrospective concise.
- Use it to make the repeatable playbook more practical.
