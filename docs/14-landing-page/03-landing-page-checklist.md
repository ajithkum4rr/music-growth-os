# Phase 14 — Landing Page Checklist

## Goal

Confirm your landing page is ready to convert visitors during the release.

## Checklist

- [ ] Headline clearly states the release or artist intent
- [ ] Supporting copy explains what the fan gets
- [ ] CTA is obvious and actionable
- [ ] Email signup form is working
- [ ] Music and social links load correctly
- [ ] Page is mobile-friendly
- [ ] Load time is acceptable
- [ ] Analytics tracking is installed
- [ ] UTM links are ready for campaign use
- [ ] Visuals match the release branding
