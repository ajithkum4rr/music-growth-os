# Phase 14 — Landing Page Guide

## Goal

Build a landing page that captures email signups and links fans to your release.

## Why this matters

A landing page is the hub for your campaign. It gives fans one place to find your music, join your list, and take action.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — choose your landing page tool

Use a simple option like:
- Carrd
- Linktree
- Beacons
- A basic website builder

### Step 2 — define the page sections

Include:
- Artist name and release headline
- Release cover image or hero visual
- Release date or launch note
- Email signup form
- Music and social links
- Short artist story or value statement

### Step 3 — add tracking

Add analytics where possible:
- Google Analytics / GA4
- Link clicks or button clicks
- UTM parameters for campaigns

### Step 4 — test the page

Verify:
- Links work correctly
- Email form submits
- The page loads fast
- Mobile layout looks good

## What to record

- Page URL
- Signup form provider
- UTM naming rules
- Test notes

## Common mistakes

- Missing an email signup.
- Linking to too many unrelated pages.
- Using vague messaging.

## Notes

- Keep the page clean and focused.
- Use the landing page as the main destination in your ads and socials.
