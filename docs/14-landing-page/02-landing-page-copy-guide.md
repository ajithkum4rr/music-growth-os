# Phase 14 — Landing Page Copy Guide

## Goal

Write landing page copy that converts visitors into listeners and subscribers.

## Why this matters

Good copy explains your release quickly and gives people a clear next step.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — write a strong headline

Use one sentence that says who you are and what the release is.
Example: `ajkay releases a cinematic new single for late-night listeners.`

### Step 2 — explain the offer

Describe what people get:
- a new song
- exclusive first listen
- email updates

### Step 3 — include supporting details

Add 2–3 short bullets:
- mood or theme of the release
- why the track matters
- what listeners should do next

### Step 4 — add a clear CTA

Use a simple action button or link:
- Listen now
- Join the email list
- Follow on Spotify

### Step 5 — keep it short

Landing page copy should be easy to skim and mobile-friendly.

## What to record

- headline idea
- subheadline or summary
- CTA language
- bullet points

## Common mistakes

- too much text.
- unclear next step.
- mixing multiple offers.

## Notes

- Use the same language as your release and social posts.
- Keep the page focused on one main action.
