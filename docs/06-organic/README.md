## Goal

Plan your organic social promotion in a way that supports discovery and audience growth.

## Why this matters

Organic strategy builds credibility, audience familiarity, and a low-cost promotion channel.

## Prerequisites

- Artist brand
- Content system
- Digital infrastructure

## Accounts & tools

- Instagram
- TikTok
- YouTube
- Email
- Content planning tool

## Estimated cost

- FREE to start.

## Time required

- 4–6 hours to plan and schedule the first content.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — choose primary platforms

Pick 2–3 platforms that match your audience and resources.

### Step 2 — define posting cadence

Plan a realistic cadence such as:
- 2–3 TikToks per week
- 2–3 Instagram posts/Reels per week
- 1 YouTube Short or video per week

### Step 3 — assign content themes

Match each platform with content types:
- TikTok: trends, hooks, audio-first clips
- Instagram: Reels, carousel stories, behind-the-scenes
- YouTube: visualizers, shorts, performance clips

### Step 4 — engage consistently

- Respond to comments.
- Follow similar artists.
- Share fan reactions.
- Save your own content to collections.

### Step 5 — monitor organic performance

Track:
- Reach
- Engagement
- Profile visits
- Link clicks

## What you should see

- A platform-focused content plan.
- A sustainable posting schedule.
- Organic performance notes.

## What to record

- Posting cadence.
- Content ideas by platform.
- Engagement notes.

## Metrics

- Reach
- Engagement rate
- Profile visits
- Share and comment behavior

## Common errors

- Posting only once or too infrequently.
- Ignoring audience feedback.
- Copying content from other artists without adaptation.

## Troubleshooting

- If a platform doesn’t respond, test a different format.
- If engagement is low, focus on hooks and stronger captions.
- If content production is too hard, simplify the format.

## Notes

- Organic growth is a long-term game.
- Use this phase to learn about your real audience before spending heavily.
