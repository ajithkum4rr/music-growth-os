# Phase 6 — Organic Content Plan

## Goal

Choose the right organic channels and create a plan for consistent launch content.

## Why this matters

Organic content builds the audience foundation, supports paid efforts, and helps with discovery on owned channels.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — choose focus platforms

Pick 2–3 platforms where your audience is most likely to be active.
Examples:
- Instagram
- TikTok
- YouTube
- Facebook

### Step 2 — define a cadence

Start with a simple posting rhythm:
- 2–3 weekly posts on the highest-priority channel
- 1–2 follow-up posts on secondary channels
- 1 email update or newsletter

### Step 3 — map content to audience stages

Plan content for:
- discovery (new listeners)
- engagement (followers)
- conversion (email signups or streams)

### Step 4 — create a content checklist

For each planned post, note:
- Format
- Hook / idea
- CTA
- Required asset

## What to record

- Platform choice rationale
- Posting cadence
- Content checklist
- Launch and follow-up ideas

## Common mistakes

- Posting everywhere at once.
- Using the same content for every platform.
- Starting without a basic plan.

## Notes

- Focus on one platform until you can sustain it.
- Organic content should support your release story and brand.
