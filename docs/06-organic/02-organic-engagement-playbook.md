# Phase 6 — Organic Engagement Playbook

## Goal

Set a practical plan for building organic momentum before and after release.

## Why this matters

Organic engagement helps your release gain credibility and keeps audiences connected without paid spend.

## Core actions

### 1. create engagement prompts

Use calls-to-action that invite participation:
- ask for playlist adds
- request fan stories
- invite comments

### 2. share behind-the-scenes

Build trust by showing the process:
- writing the song
- studio sessions
- creative inspiration

### 3. engage directly

Reply to comments and messages.
Recognize fans and encourage sharing.

### 4. use community spaces

Post in relevant groups, forums, or fan communities.
Keep the content helpful, not spammy.

### 5. measure signal

Track engagement by:
- comments and replies
- shares and saves
- story interactions

## Checklist

- [ ] engagement prompts planned
- [ ] behind-the-scenes content created
- [ ] community spaces identified
- [ ] response routine established
- [ ] engagement results tracked

## Notes

- Consistency beats one-off posts.
- Align organic activity with release milestones.
