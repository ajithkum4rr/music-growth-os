# Phase 6 — Organic Content Distribution Guide

## Goal

Distribute organic content efficiently across the platforms that matter most.

## Why this matters

Even great organic content needs a distribution plan to be seen by the right audience.

## Distribution steps

### 1. identify priority channels

Choose 2–3 channels where your audience spends attention:
- Instagram
- TikTok
- YouTube
- email
- playlists

### 2. tailor each post

Adjust copy and assets for each channel’s format and audience behavior.

### 3. batch schedule

Use a single schedule for organic posts and community shares.

### 4. reuse high-performing content

Repurpose what works into new formats and channels.

### 5. monitor and refine

Track what gains the most engagement and double down.

## Checklist

- [ ] priority channels chosen
- [ ] content adapted for each platform
- [ ] publish schedule created
- [ ] repurposing plan drafted
- [ ] engagement monitored

## Notes

- Keep the distribution plan modest so it stays sustainable.
- Focus on consistency instead of chasing every new platform.
