# Phase 25 — Post-Campaign Review

## Goal

Capture what worked, what didn’t, and what should change for the next release.

## Why this matters

A strong post-campaign review turns experience into actionable learning and makes the next launch more effective.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — collect final results

Gather results for:
- streams and followers
- email growth
- landing page conversions
- ad performance
- social engagement

### Step 2 — compare to expectations

Note where you hit or missed your targets.
Record what surprised you.

### Step 3 — write lessons learned

Answer:
- what worked well?
- what needed improvement?
- what should we test next?

### Step 4 — update the playbook

Add the review notes to your repeatable playbook.
Include:
- successful tactics
- failed experiments
- timeline changes

## What to record

- campaign results summary
- lessons learned
- next release recommendations

## Common mistakes

- treating the review as optional.
- only focusing on metrics.
- failing to document why decisions were made.

## Notes

- Use this review to improve both strategy and execution.
- Keep it honest and specific.
