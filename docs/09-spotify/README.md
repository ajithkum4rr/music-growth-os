## Goal

Optimize Spotify presence and measure performance.

## Why this matters

Spotify is often the primary destination for streams, followers, and algorithmic discovery.

## Prerequisites

- Spotify for Artists access
- Release metadata ready
- Analytics tracking plan

## Accounts & tools

- Spotify for Artists
- Distributor dashboard
- Internal notes or tracker

## Estimated cost

- FREE.

## Time required

- 3–5 hours to complete profile setup and metric tracking.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — claim Spotify for Artists

If you haven’t already:
- request access at artists.spotify.com
- verify via distributor or record label
- confirm profile ownership

### Step 2 — optimize your profile

- Add an artist bio
- Add images and cover art
- Update links and social handles
- Choose an Artist Pick if available
- Add Canvas visuals if available

### Step 3 — understand Spotify metrics

Track:
- Monthly listeners
- Streams
- Saves
- Followers
- Playlist adds
- Source of streams
- Discovery modes
- Listener demographics
- Locations

### Step 4 — plan engagement and release signals

- Encourage saves and follows in captions and CTAs
- Use playlist pitching notes
- Track repeat listening and playlist performance

### Step 5 — document metric sources

- Spotify for Artists dashboard
- Release analytics pages
- Weekly performance notes

## What you should see

- A completed Spotify profile.
- Key metrics tracked.
- A plan for artist pitching and playlists.

## What to record

- Spotify profile changes.
- Weekly stream and follower numbers.
- Discovery source notes.

## Metrics

- Monthly listeners
- Streams
- Saves
- Followers
- Playlist additions
- Repeat listeners

## Common errors

- Leaving bio or images blank.
- Not tracking discovery source trends.
- Not asking for saves or follows.

## Troubleshooting

- If metrics are low, review your release pitch and playlists.
- If playlist adds are weak, refine your outreach targets.
- If followers lag, improve profile conversion in posts.

## Notes

- Spotify data is the most direct signal of listener behavior.
- VERIFY CURRENT Spotify UI and menu names.
