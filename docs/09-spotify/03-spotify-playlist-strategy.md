# Phase 9 — Spotify Playlist Strategy

## Goal

Develop a clear playlist strategy for release promotion and discovery.

## Why this matters

Playlists are a major source of streams and new listeners. Strategy helps you target the right placements.

## Strategy elements

### 1. define target playlist types

Choose playlists that match your sound:
- editorial playlists
- algorithmic playlists
- independent curator playlists
- mood / theme playlists

### 2. prioritize by relevance

Focus on playlists with audiences most likely to become fans, not just the biggest followings.

### 3. align pitch messaging

Use a release angle that fits each playlist’s vibe.

### 4. schedule outreach

Stagger outreach so follow-ups are timely and you can track responses.

### 5. track results

Record which playlists add the song and the listening impact.

## Checklist

- [ ] playlist types defined
- [ ] target list prioritized
- [ ] pitch messaging aligned
- [ ] outreach scheduled
- [ ] results tracked

## Notes

- Treat playlists as both traffic and social proof.
- Update your strategy as you learn which playlists respond best.
