# Phase 9 — Spotify Optimization

## Goal

Optimize your Spotify presence to support discovery and performance tracking.

## Why this matters

Spotify is a primary streaming destination. A strong profile and analytics setup helps you understand listeners and improve future releases.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — claim and verify Spotify for Artists

- Confirm you have access to your artist profile.
- Verify the profile picture, bio, and links.
- Add team members or managers if needed.

### Step 2 — optimize your artist profile

- Write a short bio that reflects your brand.
- Add a profile image and banner.
- Choose the correct artist genre and location settings.

### Step 3 — prepare Spotify assets

- Save the release date in your calendar.
- Prepare the Spotify pitch text.
- Add Canvas or promotional visuals if available.

### Step 4 — review analytics signals

- Note your current baseline for monthly listeners.
- Track saves, streams, and follower growth.
- Watch for playlist and referral source changes.

## What to record

- Spotify profile URL.
- Artist bio and imagery notes.
- Pitch submission copy.
- Baseline metrics.

## Common mistakes

- Leaving the bio generic.
- Missing the artist profile image.
- Failing to log baseline metrics before launch.

## Notes

- Use your brand language consistently.
- Focus on real user engagement, not playlist counts.
