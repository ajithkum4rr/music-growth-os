# Phase 21 — Optimization Plan

## Goal

Turn campaign performance into concrete optimization actions.

## Why this matters

Optimization helps you improve results with the same resources by focusing on the highest-impact changes.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — review the funnel

Look at each stage:
- awareness
- engagement
- conversion
- retention

### Step 2 — identify the weakest step

Choose one area to improve first, such as:
- low ad click-through rate
- poor email open rate
- weak landing page conversions

### Step 3 — form a hypothesis

Write a short test statement:
- If we change X, then Y will improve.

### Step 4 — run a small test

Keep tests simple:
- change one creative
- adjust one audience
- refine one headline

### Step 5 — document the result

Note:
- what you tested
- the outcome
- whether to keep or discard it

## What to record

- Optimization hypothesis
- Test plan
- Results
- Next step

## Common mistakes

- changing too many things at once.
- optimizing without a baseline.
- ignoring learnings from the test.

## Notes

- Small improvements compound over time.
- Use real data before scaling changes.
