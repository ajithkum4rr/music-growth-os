# Phase 26 — Repeatable Playbook

## Goal

Turn this launch into a documented playbook you can use again and again.

## Why this matters

A repeatable playbook saves time, keeps quality consistent, and makes future releases more predictable.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — summarize the launch flow

Write the key steps from planning to post-launch review.
Include:
- audience research
- content and campaign planning
- launch execution
- analytics and optimization

### Step 2 — capture templates and checklists

Collect the tools that worked:
- content matrix
- ad plan
- email workflow
- review checklist

### Step 3 — document what changed

Note any adaptations you made for this release:
- timing changes
- channel priorities
- audience shifts

### Step 4 — make it reusable

Write clear next-release instructions:
- what to keep the same
- what to improve
- what to test next time

## What to record

- launch flow summary
- working templates
- recommended improvements
- next-release plan

## Common mistakes

- leaving the playbook incomplete.
- copying the same plan without adapting.
- forgetting to update the playbook after each launch.

## Notes

- A good playbook is short, clear, and practical.
- Update it after every release.
