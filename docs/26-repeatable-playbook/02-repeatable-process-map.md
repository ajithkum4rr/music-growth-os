# Phase 26 — Repeatable Process Map

## Goal

Capture the repeatable process for launching music in a compact, visual way.

## Why this matters

A process map makes it easier to repeat the playbook and onboard collaborators.

## Process map sections

### 1. preparation

- brand and positioning
- audience research
- release planning
- content and infrastructure setup

### 2. launch

- landing page activation
- email and social roll-out
- playlist and PR outreach
- paid and organic promotion

### 3. optimization

- performance monitoring
- test planning
- campaign tuning

### 4. retention

- email and fan follow-up
- community engagement
- repeat release planning

## How to use it

- review before each release
- use as a quick checklist for the team
- update it after every major campaign

## Notes

- Keep the map concise and aligned to your actual workflow.
- Use it to share the release blueprint with collaborators.
