# Phase 2 — Competitor Scan

## Goal

Learn from similar artists so you can spot audience, content, and campaign signals.

## Why this matters

Competitor analysis helps you avoid guesswork and identify practical inspiration.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — choose 3–5 reference artists

Pick artists who:
- Sound similar to `ajkay`
- Share a comparable mood or audience
- Have visible organic traction or playlists

### Step 2 — scan their platforms

For each artist, note:
- Top platforms they post on
- Types of content they create
- Visual style and tone
- Fan engagement patterns

### Step 3 — identify audience signals

Note:
- What kind of fans comment or share
- Whether they use Reels, Stories, or short-form clips
- If they promote playlists or email signups
- How they show the song or release story

### Step 4 — capture the lessons

Record:
- What you can adapt
- What you want to avoid
- What feels strong or weak

## What to record

- Artist list and why you chose them.
- Platform and content notes.
- Audience signals and inspiration.

## Common mistakes

- Copying exact content formats.
- Focusing only on big names.
- Missing the audience intent behind the content.

## Notes

- Use this analysis as direction, not imitation.
- The goal is to learn from their strategy and adapt it to your brand.
