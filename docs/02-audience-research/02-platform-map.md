# Phase 2 — Platform Mapping

## Goal

Map where your target listeners spend time and how they discover music.

## Why this matters

Knowing platform behaviour helps you choose where to publish content and where to advertise.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — list relevant platforms

Common platforms:
- Instagram
- TikTok
- YouTube
- Spotify
- Facebook
- Email
- Blogs and playlists

### Step 2 — rate each platform for each archetype

For each archetype, record:
- Primary platform
- Secondary platform
- Best content format
- Discovery mode (search, trends, playlists, ads)

### Step 3 — note platform signals

Write down what type of content performs well:
- Short-form video
- Static artwork
- Stories and Reels
- Behind-the-scenes
- Audio previews
- Playlist recommendations

### Step 4 — prioritize your first 3 platforms

Based on the mapping, choose the first platforms to focus on.
Keep it small and aligned with your archetypes.

## What to record

- Platform-selection notes.
- Prioritized channels.
- Content format recommendations.

## Common mistakes

- Choosing every platform at once.
- Ignoring the platform’s core content type.
- Assuming a platform is good just because it is popular.

## Notes

- Start with the platforms that match your archetypes.
- Revisit this map after first results.
