# Phase 2 — Audience Archetypes

## Goal

Define 3–5 listener archetypes for `ajkay`.

## Why this matters

Audience archetypes make targeting, content, and messaging much more precise.
When you know who you are talking to, your campaigns become clearer.

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — choose your top archetypes

Start with 3 archetypes, such as:
- Late-night listeners
- Bedroom producers
- Chill playlist seekers
- Emotional pop fans
- Cinematic audio listeners

### Step 2 — describe each archetype

For each archetype, record:
- Name
- Age range
- Interests
- Preferred platforms
- Listening situation
- Why they would connect with your music

### Step 3 — map platform behaviour

For each archetype, note:
- Primary platforms (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Spotify)
- Content they consume
- How they discover new music
- What type of creative catches their attention

## What to record

- Archetype names and descriptions.
- Platform behaviour for each archetype.
- Hypotheses about how they discover music.

## Common mistakes

- Making archetypes too broad.
- Assuming everyone is the same.
- Choosing platforms without evidence.

## Notes

- These are hypotheses, not data facts.
- Use available research and listener intuition.
- Refine as you learn from actual performance.
