# Glossary

## Analytics terms
- Conversion rate: percentage of visitors who complete a desired action.
- CPM: cost per thousand impressions.
- CPC: cost per click.
- CTR: click-through rate.
- Lifetime value: long-term value of a fan or customer.

## Music marketing terms
- Artist positioning: the statement that explains who you are and who you are for.
- Release playbook: the documented process for launching music.
- Playlist outreach: contacting playlist curators or playlist owners.
- PR outreach: contacting media, blogs, podcasts, and press.

## Platform terms
- Meta Business Manager: Facebook’s tool for managing Pages, ad accounts, and assets.
- Spotify for Artists: Spotify’s dashboard for artists and managers.
- UTM: tracking parameters added to URLs to measure source and campaign.
- Pixel: a tracking snippet used to capture actions on a website or landing page.

## Funnel stages
- Cold audience: people who have not yet interacted with your content.
- Warm audience: people who have shown some interest or engagement.
- Hot audience: people who are close to taking action.
- Fan: a repeat listener, follower, or email subscriber.
